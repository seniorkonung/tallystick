### Product context

`docs/product.md` describes the product: the problem it solves, its users, principles, non-goals, and settled product decisions. Read it before product, UX, or requirements work, including prototype features and OpenSpec proposals and specs.

When the user settles a product decision or rejects an alternative, propose recording it in `docs/product.md`.
